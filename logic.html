Course Code: PHIL 1009

Logic & Critical Thinking

Comprehensive lecture modules, formal argumentation diagrams, fallacy guides, and module handouts for freshman engineering and science students.

Module 01

Chapter 1: Defining Philosophy

Origins, definitions, core branches (Metaphysics, Epistemology, Axiology, Logic), and the fundamental philosophical attitudes.

View Lecture → Study Notes
Module 02

Chapter 2: Basic Argument Concepts

Premises and conclusions, recognizing arguments, deductive vs inductive arguments, validity, truth, and soundness.

View Lecture → Study Notes
Module 03

Chapter 3: Logic and Language

Cognitive vs emotive meanings, types of definitions, intensional vs extensional definitions, and criteria for lexical definitions.

View Lecture → Study Notes
Module 04

Chapter 4: Critical Thinking Concepts

Standards of critical thinking, barriers to critical thought (egocentrism, sociocentrism, relativistic thinking), and cognitive biases.

View Lecture → Study Notes
Module 05

Chapter 5: Informal Fallacies

Fallacies of relevance, weak induction, presumption, ambiguity, and grammatical analogy. Diagnostic examples and exercises.

View Lecture → Study Notes
Module 06

Chapter 6: Categorical Propositions

Standard form categorical statements (A, E, I, O), Venn diagrams, traditional square of opposition, and categorical syllogisms.

View Lecture → Study Notes
