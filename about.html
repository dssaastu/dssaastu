Our History

Rooted in academic excellence since the establishment of AASTU, our department serves as the intellectual crucible where science, technology, human ethics, and civic responsibility intersect.

Our Mission

To deliver high-impact humanistic and social education equipping engineers and scientists with critical reasoning, constitutional awareness, and ethical perspectives required to solve complex societal problems.

Our Vision

To be an African center of excellence recognized for integrating social sciences with STEM education, advancing interdisciplinary research, and nurturing patriotic, globally competitive innovators.
