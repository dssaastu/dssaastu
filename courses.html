PHIL.1009

Logic & Critical Thinking

Foundational study of deductive and inductive logic, structure of arguments, categorical propositions, standard Venn diagram representations, traditional square of opposition, and fallacies.

Course Portal Chapter 1 PDF Notes
MCIE.1012

Moral & Civic Education

Exploration of philosophical and applied ethics, moral theories, constitutional law, human rights, civic participation, state structures, and democratic governance in Ethiopia.

Course Portal Module 1 Interactive Quiz
GeIs.1001

Geography of Ethiopia & the Horn

Physical, socioeconomic, and environmental geography of the Horn of Africa, spatial analysis, topography, hydrological systems including the Nile Basin, and resource management.

Related Research Inquire Syllabus
Hist.1001

History of Ethiopia & the Horn

Longue-durée historical developments from ancient human origins, state formations, trade routes, religious dynamics, anti-colonial resistance (Battle of Adwa), and modern state building.

Soc.1001

Social Anthropology

Comprehensive survey of sociocultural anthropological concepts, kinship and marriage systems, customary conflict resolution, cultural relativism, and indigenous heritage preservation.

GlTr.1001

Global Trends & Geopolitics

Analysis of changing global orders, multipolarity, international organizations, climate change diplomacy, African continental trade (AfCFTA), and the political economy of the Red Sea region.
